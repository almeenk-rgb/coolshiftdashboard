Why a retrofit layer

BEE's ECSBC 2024 (section 3.2.5) applies to new HVAC equipment; existing systems are exempt. Buildings use about 33% of India's electricity (Challenge 02 brief), so the installed base needs an add-on route to savings.

First reference: IIIT-Delhi Academic Building, with I-BLEND 2014–2017 mains, Wi-Fi and calendar data. To transfer, recalibrate weather (Navi Mumbai next), occupancy, AHU mapping, comfort rules and tariff.
